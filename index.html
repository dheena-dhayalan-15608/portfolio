From circuits to services

I studied Electrical and Electronics Engineering at R.M.D. Engineering College, affiliated by Anna University, then moved into software through Zoho Schools of Graduate Studies. Since September 2022 I’ve been a Member Technical Staff on Zoho SalesIQ, working on the backend.

Day to day that means Java services, JSP and Servlets, MySQL, Redis, Kafka and Cassandra, and lately, wiring large language models into product features.

The problems I enjoy most are the ones where the answer is a design decision rather than more code: what to cache, what to make asynchronous, and how to check what a model sends back before a customer ever sees it.

What I work with

The tools I reach for most, and what each one is for in my work.

  • Java

    My everyday language: services and request handling across SalesIQ’s backend.

  • JSP and Servlets

    HTTP endpoints and server-rendered pages, close to the request lifecycle.

  • MySQL

    Relational data: schemas, indexes, and queries that stay fast as tables grow.

  • Redis

    Caching hot data so the database isn’t hit on every request.

  • Kafka

    Passing events between services so neither has to wait on the other.

  • Cassandra

    Write-heavy data spread across nodes, with replication built in.

  • LLM integration

    Connecting language models to product features, and checking what they send back.

What’s happening

    Two producers publish events to a Kafka topic with three partitions. Events with the same key always land in the same partition, so their order holds. Two consumers read in parallel and write to a four-node Cassandra ring that keeps two copies of every row.
